Your care cards and iCloud

Care cards are saved on your device first, so you can use them when iCloud is unavailable. Optional private iCloud sync keeps them available on your devices signed in to the same Apple Account. Pet Handoff does not require a separate account.

If sync needs attention, open Pet Handoff Settings and tap Retry. If you need to enable iCloud, open the Settings app, tap your name, then iCloud. Return to Pet Handoff when iCloud is available; sync will retry automatically.

Pet Handoff Pro and purchases

Pro is a one-time purchase through the App Store, with no subscription. It unlocks unlimited pets and PDF or image exports, removes the export watermark, and lets you add pet photos. The free version includes one pet, one watermarked PDF or image export, and unlimited plain-text sharing.

To restore a purchase, open Settings > Upgrade > Restore Purchases, or use Restore Purchases on the Pro screen. Use the same Apple Account used for the purchase. If the purchase is not found or Pro remains locked, try again later and contact support.

Previewing and sharing a handoff

Open a pet, choose Preview handoff, then copy plain text or choose Share PDF or Share image. iOS shows the system share sheet so you can select the destination. Review the handoff before sending it; the destination you choose receives the information included in it.
